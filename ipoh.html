D2
⛩ Cave Temple Day
Sat 20 Jun · Gunung Rapat Limestone Hills
▾
🍳
Hotel Breakfast
Breakfast included for 2 guests/room — good start before the temple walk
⛩
Sam Poh Tong Cave Temple
One of Malaysia's largest cave temples — beautiful Chinese Buddhist shrines set into the limestone, turtle ponds, gardens painted in reds & oranges
🚗 Grab ~10–15 min south of hotel · Jalan Gopeng
🌊
Tasik Cermin (Mirror Lake)
Hidden gem near Sam Poh Tong — walk through a limestone tunnel to emerge at a serene turquoise lake · wild otters sometimes spotted! · MYR 12/person entry
🚶 5 min walk from Sam Poh Tong
🛕
Kek Lok Tong Cave Temple
A few minutes away — 'Cave of Great Happiness' — beautiful open gardens inside the limestone cave, peaceful walk-through. Free entry!
🚗 2 min from Sam Poh Tong
🍱
Lunch — Ipoh Old Town
Head back for dim sum at Lou Wong (bean sprout chicken) or Foh San (famous dim sum)
🚗 Grab back to Old Town ~15 min
🎨
Mural Art's Lane + Heritage Walk
Ernest Zacharevic murals · Heritage Trail starts at Ipoh Railway Station · Concubine Lane in daylight · 22 Hale Street museum & café
🛍
Kong Heng Flea Market
Creative crafts bazaar behind Plan B restaurant · open daily 10am–5pm · great for souvenirs
✨ Also Consider
🗿
Perak Tong Cave Temple
On the way back — 12m Buddha statue inside the cave + 385 steps to the top for panoramic views of Ipoh. A workout but worth it!
🌆
Ipoh Railway Station (Daytime)
1917 colonial Moorish architecture — Heritage Trail starts here with a map of all key old town sites. Great for photos.
🥧
Egg Custard Tarts (Heong Peng)
Ipoh is famous for its egg tarts — grab some from Caely Cake House or Sin Eng Heong. Kids will love them.
🐟
Salted Chicken (Ayam Sejuk)
Another Ipoh icon — poached salted chicken served cold, silky smooth. Best from Restoran Onn Kee or the Old Town area.

🎢 Lost World Day
Sun 21 Jun · Lost World of Tambun
▾
🍳
Hotel Breakfast
Early start — Lost World opens at 11am, arrive as early as possible
🎢
Lost World of Tambun
40-acre theme + water park set among 400-million-year-old limestone hills · slides, lazy river, wave pool, petting zoo, amusement rides · great for Ethan & Jie Sheng!
🚗 Grab ~20 min from hotel · open 11am–6pm · bring swimwear!
🍔
Lunch at Lost World
Food stalls inside the park — expect Malaysian staples and snacks
🛍
Memory Lane Flea Market (Sundays only!)
If energy allows after Lost World — Sunday market fills an entire street with vintage records, antiques, local crafts · best in the morning so may need to swap order
🚗 Old Town area · consider doing this first (9am–1pm), then Lost World
🍜
Dinner — Last Ipoh Feast
Return to Old Town for a proper Ipoh dinner — char kuey teow, curry mee, or roast pork rice
✨ Also Consider
🌿
Lost World Petting Zoo / Night Zoo
Lost World has a petting zoo and night zoo (open 7–10pm) — great extra option if kids have energy left after the water park!
🍡
Tambun Pomelo
Tambun is famous for its sweet, juicy pomelos — pick some up near Lost World, they make great snacks and gifts
☕
White Coffee Night Cap
End the evening with a classic Ipoh white coffee at the Old Town Kopitiam — the kids can have Milo ais while the adults enjoy the coffee ritual

🚗 Getting Around
🚗
Grab (recommended)
Main app for taxis in Ipoh. Most rides within town ~RM 8–15. Airport to hotel ~RM 25–35. Book via Grab app.
🚶
Old Town is walkable
Concubine Lane, murals, Railway Station and the flea market are all within easy walking distance of each other.
🛻
Rental car (optional)
If you want flexibility for cave temples + Tambun on the same day, a rental car from the airport is ~RM 100–150/day. Driving in Ipoh is easy.
